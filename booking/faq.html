Frequently asked questions

How do I book?
Book online: choose your treatment, a physio and a time, and pay a small deposit by card. Or call us on 0117 496 0418 or email hello@larkspire-physio.example. We answer the phone Monday to Saturday.
Do I need to pay a deposit?
When you book online you pay a deposit by card. It is £15 to £40, depending on the treatment (see our prices). Your appointment is confirmed when the deposit is paid, and the deposit is taken off your bill on the day. You pay the rest at the end of your appointment, by card or cash.
Can I cancel or move my appointment?
Yes. Cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment starts and we refund your deposit in full. If you cancel later than that, or do not come, we keep the deposit. You can cancel with the link in your confirmation email, or call us on 0117 496 0418. To move your appointment, cancel it and book a new time.
What should I wear?
Something comfortable that lets your physio see and move the area that hurts. Shorts are useful for knee and hip problems.
Do I need a GP referral?
No. You can book directly. If you are claiming on health insurance, check with your insurer first.
What do you do with my details?
We use your name, email address and phone number only to manage your appointments and to contact you about them. Please do not send us medical details by email: your physio will talk with you about your health at your first appointment.

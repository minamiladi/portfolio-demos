Our team

Two chartered physiotherapists, each with their own treatment room. Maren leads our acupuncture clinic and Idris leads sports injuries and running gait analysis.

Maren Lockwood

Senior Physiotherapist (MCSP)

Maren has worked in musculoskeletal physiotherapy for 14 years and leads our acupuncture clinic. She treats back and neck pain, shoulder problems and rehabilitation after surgery.

Treatments: initial assessment, follow-up treatment, acupuncture and sports massage.

Works on: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri (Room 1).

Idris Penhaligon

Sports Physiotherapist (MCSP)

Idris works with runners, cyclists and club rugby players. He runs our gait analysis lab and the Saturday sports clinic.

Treatments: initial assessment, follow-up treatment, sports injury assessment, sports massage and running gait analysis.

Works on: Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat (Room 2).

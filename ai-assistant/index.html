Car rental in Jeddah, made simple

About us

Sanbouk Drive has rented cars in Jeddah since 2016. We have two branches, at King Abdulaziz International Airport (Terminal 1) and in Al-Rawdah, and a fleet of more than 300 cars, none older than three years.

How to book

Book on this website, call +966 12 000 0140 (daily 8:00–23:00), send a WhatsApp message to +966 12 000 0141, or visit one of our branches.

Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, mada and Apple Pay. Cash is accepted at our branches for the rental amount only, not for the deposit.

Self-drive only

All our rentals are self-drive. We do not offer cars with a driver.

Roadside assistance

Every rental includes roadside assistance 24 hours a day: +966 12 000 0199.

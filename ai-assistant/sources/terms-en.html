Rental Terms

Sanbouk Drive: Rental Terms and Refund Policy (version 3, October 2026)

introThese terms apply to every rental contract with Sanbouk Drive Car Rental Co..

1. Who can rent

1.1The renter must be at least 21 years old (25 for the Luxury class).

1.2The renter must have held a driving licence for at least 12 months.

1.3Saudi citizens and residents: national ID or Iqama and a valid Saudi driving licence.

1.4Visitors: passport, valid visa, a driving licence from their country and an International Driving Permit.

2. Booking and payment

2.1Bookings can be made on our website, through the call centre, on WhatsApp or at a branch.

2.2We accept Visa, Mastercard, mada and Apple Pay. Cash is accepted at the branches for the rental amount only.

3. Security deposit

3.1A security deposit is blocked on a credit card at pick-up: SAR 1,000 for Economy and Sedan, SAR 2,000 for SUV and Van, and SAR 5,000 for Luxury.

3.2If you pay the deposit with a mada debit card, it is charged and then refunded after the car is returned.

3.3The deposit is released within 14 days after the car is returned, once traffic fines have been checked.

4. Kilometres and fuel

4.1Daily rentals include 300 km per day, weekly rentals 2,000 km and monthly rentals 4,500 km. Extra kilometres are charged as shown on the rate card.

4.2Full to full: the car is delivered with a full tank and must be returned full. Missing fuel is charged at the pump price plus a SAR 30 service fee.

5. Late return

5.1There is a grace period of 60 minutes.

5.2After the grace period, each extra hour is charged at 20% of the daily rate.

5.3If the car is more than 4 hours late, one full extra day is charged.

6. Cancellation and refunds

6.1Cancellation is free of charge up to 48 hours before the pick-up time.

6.2If you cancel less than 48 hours before the pick-up time, one day's rent is charged.

6.3No-show: if you do not come within 6 hours of the pick-up time, the booking is cancelled and one day's rent is charged.

6.4Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7 to 14 working days.

6.5Early return: we recalculate the rent at the rate for the days used; if this is less than what you paid, we refund the difference.

7. Insurance cover

7.1Every rental includes basic cover: third-party liability and damage to the rental car, with an excess of SAR 2,500 (SAR 7,500 for Luxury).

7.2Full cover (zero excess) is optional; its daily price is on the rate card.

7.3Cover does not apply without an official accident report, when driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, when driving off-road, or when the driver is not named in the contract.

8. Accidents and breakdowns

8.1Call our roadside assistance, 24 hours a day: +966 12 000 0199.

8.2If anyone is injured or the road is blocked, call 911 first.

8.3Do not leave the scene of an accident, and get an official accident report.

8.4We send a replacement car within 4 hours inside Jeddah.

9. Where you can drive

9.1The car may be driven anywhere inside Saudi Arabia and must be returned in Jeddah.

9.2The car may not be driven outside Saudi Arabia (for example to Bahrain, the UAE or Jordan).

10. Traffic fines

10.1Traffic fines during the rental are charged to the customer, plus an admin fee of SAR 25 per fine.

11. Smoking and cleaning

11.1Smoking is not allowed in the car. A cleaning fee of SAR 300 applies.

12. Personal data

12.1We use your personal data only to complete your rental and do not share it with anyone except as required by law.

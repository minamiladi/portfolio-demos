# Larkspire Physiotherapy: online booking (demo project)

A fictional clinic, not a real business. This is a static, clickable copy of the booking site for a portfolio:

- The free times come from the real app's own slot engine (UK time with GMT/BST, bank holidays, the lunch break, a 10-minute gap after each appointment), made in advance up to 31 March 2027.
- Bookings live only in your browser tab. The payment step is a neutral demo step: no card details are asked for and nothing is charged.
- The confirmation email, the HubSpot records and the Google Analytics events are shown on the page, simulated. Nothing is sent anywhere.

In the live version the deposit is taken with Stripe Checkout (test mode in the portfolio run).
